What lilliss.au says that jaredlilliss.github.io does not

  • An offer with a scopeFive named services and who they are for: hospitality venues and small businesses, often mid-service.
  • A place and a stateBass Hill, Sydney and regional NSW. Taking new work. The personal site has a city; the business has a service area and a status.
  • A trading identityTrading since 2019, registered 2026, ABN 78 576 434 613. Facts a customer or an accountant needs and a portfolio reader does not.
  • A promise about handoffsOne person, no handoffs: the person who takes the call scopes it, installs it, documents it, and answers when it breaks.
  • A way to engageA business address and a contact form of intent (the fault, the gear, the urgency, the suburb). Whether a phone number joins it is the owner’s decision.
  • A privacy page for a businessWhat is collected when someone writes in, and what is not. The personal site’s privacy page speaks for a portfolio.

What the two sites may share

  • SharedThe person. One name, one face, one GitHub and one LinkedIn. Two sites, one Jared.
  • SharedThe receipts ethic, in one sentence: what broke, what fixed it, what to watch for next time. It is the reason to trust either site.
  • SharedThe Aussie Health Utility card — the same facts on both sites, framed as work on one and as proof of reliability on the other.
  • SharedThe privacy page skeleton and the footer legal line. Boring components should be identical; only their dress changes.
  • Not sharedPalette, typefaces, hero, voice, nav order. A visitor who lands on both should feel two different rooms in the same house.
