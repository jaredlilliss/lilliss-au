The proposition

Four programmes, one ecosystem

XperienceSpace comprehensive set of programs is designed to establish, expand and support the local ecosystem supporting the UAE vision for space.

  1. 01Community Engagement
  2. 02Education & Talent Pipeline
  3. 03Industry Development & Support
  4. 04Research & Development

Program development incentivises industry and R&D to locate to UAE, driving the transfer of technology and talent to the region.

About this concept

This page is a design exercise: the initiative’s own published words, set in a register that says what the tills-down trade says on lilliss.au — one colour, black type, a figure you can read from across the road. Every sentence in quotation marks is quoted from xperiencespace.com; the section titles and the one line of framing under the headline are the author’s.

No Arabic edition is offered here. The initiative’s site carries a substantial Arabic edition of its own; an Arabic edition of this concept would be issued right-to-left by a translator, and it is not faked with a machine translation. The one Arabic word below is quoted, not translated.
