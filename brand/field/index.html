What I do

Mostly hospitality venues and small businesses — often mid-service, when it can’t wait until Monday.

  1. POS & EFTPOS

    Rollouts, replacements and fault resolution for point-of-sale and payment terminals. Getting the tills taking money again is the job.

  2. Windows support

    Endpoint and hardware support for Windows machines — setup, repair, migration, and the recurring faults nobody has had time to chase down.

  3. Networks

    Structured cabling, switching and Wi-Fi for small sites. Tidy racks, labelled runs, and documentation you can hand to the next person.

  4. CCTV & AV

    Camera and audio-visual installation and troubleshooting for venues and shopfronts, from mounting to configuration.

  5. Small-business IT

    The rest of it — email, printers, backups, vendor wrangling. If it plugs in and the business depends on it, it’s in scope.

A fault isn’t fixed when it stops showing. It’s fixed when it’s written down — what broke, what fixed it, and what to watch for next time.

Aussie Health Utility

A collector that takes a reading from every NSW emergency department publishing a live count, every fifteen minutes since July 2026. It runs unattended on a small Melbourne server, backs itself up nightly, and publishes a board anyone can read — with the counting method written on the page, including what the data cannot support.

Field tech hands, systems head

Lilliss IT is a one-man operation, on purpose. No handoffs between a sales rep, a dispatcher and a tech who’s never seen your site — the person you first speak to is the person on the ladder.

Day work keeps the standard honest. When a venue’s tills stop mid-service the fix has to be real, fast, and documented, because the next person to touch that site deserves to know what happened.

Away from client work I build a privacy-by-design data platform over public Australian health datasets — a polling loop feeding an append-only PostgreSQL time series, with the privacy posture set at the schema level so no personal or health information is ever collected. It has been collecting since July 2026, and now runs unattended on a Melbourne server. See the build →
