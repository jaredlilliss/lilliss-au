The First Space Nation

UAE’s long-term vision for the future

Guided by visionary leadership, the UAE is transitioning from a resourced based to a hiTech economy driven by the space program. Early achievements have helped form a structured, long-horizon strategy with advanced missions, strong governance, and a developing space sector. This strategy propels UAE towards leadership in Exploration, R&D and Investment developing exciting opportunties for the global space economy.

From historic vision to structured roadmap

The UAE’s interest in astronomy and space sciences dates back to the 1970s, when Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan met the NASA team responsible for the Apollo Moon landing. Under the visionary leadership of UAE government this vision evolved into a clear mandate: Position the UAE as a global leader in Space Exploration, Research & Investment.

The UAE now advances sovereign missions, agreed in January 2024 to provide the Lunar Gateway’s Crew and Science Airlock, and invests in technologies, governance and infrastructure that enable long-term human presence beyond Earth.

Why an ecosystem is essential

To sustain this trajectory, the UAE requires a robust local ecosystem which develops talent, aligns regulations, attracts private capital, and accelerates technology transfer into the country.

XperienceSpace complements UAE's vision by providing a structured collaboration platform which develops industry & community, making UAE’s long-term space vision economically tangible and globally accessible.
