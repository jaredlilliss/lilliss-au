XperienceSpace · an independent initiative

Building the UAE Space Nation

An unsolicited design concept by Jared Lilliss (Lilliss IT), Australia. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or commissioned by the XperienceSpace Initiative. Every UAE fact below is sourced to the body that published it.

The UAE is moving from a resource-based economy to a high-technology one, with the space programme as its engine. What follows separates two things that are easy to blur: what the country has already done, which is a matter of record, and what XperienceSpace proposes to build alongside it, which is a proposal.

01 · The record

What the UAE has already done

Every line belongs to the UAE government or one of its agencies, with the owner named. Where a fact has changed since it was published, the change is stated rather than dropped — which is the part a reader who knows the programme notices first.

02 · Core framework

Four pillars powering XperienceSpace

The initiative structures its programmes around four interdependent pillars. Each channels community energy, builds capability, and supports the UAE’s official space initiatives with practical, investment-ready mechanisms.

01

Public engagement & community

Raising awareness and inspiring innovation by addressing Earth’s challenges, creating new opportunities for the UAE’s growth.

02

Education & talent pipeline

Developing future leaders by applying UAE-driven solutions to global and space challenges, strengthening national expertise.

03

Industry development & support

Creating initiatives that develop cutting-edge solutions for local and global needs, driving the UAE’s leadership in space.

04

Research & development

Developing the products and services needed to solve Earth’s sustainability challenges and build a competitive, future-ready sector.

03 · Ecosystem

Making the vision investable, practical and inclusive

The XperienceSpace ecosystem connects community, academia, industry, investors and government stakeholders into one platform, designed to attract technology, talent and capital into the UAE.

The four items below are concept programmes proposed by XperienceSpace, an independent industry initiative. They are not UAE government programmes, and this page is not affiliated with the UAE Space Agency, MBRSC, or any UAE government body.

The UAE’s own space economic zone is the UAE Space Agency’s Space Economic Zones Programme, first zone at Masdar City. “UAE Space City” below is a proposal, and is not that programme.

UAE Space Community
A national movement where youth, families, professionals and creators take part in UAE space initiatives through digital platforms, outreach programmes, events, challenges and learning pathways.
UAE Space City
A scalable, future-ready urban and economic zone housing R&D centres, manufacturing clusters, training facilities and high-technology workplaces dedicated to space and sustainability.
UAE Space Welcome Center
The gateway at the heart of the proposed city: immersive exhibits, simulators, labs, events and industry showcases linking the public to live missions.
Future Development Funds
A series of high-technology funds channelling private and institutional capital into UAE-based solutions.
04 · Strategic trajectory

From programmes to a functioning space city

A phased roadmap converting ambition into visible assets — supporting UAE initiatives, de-risking investment, and aligning ecosystem maturity with national milestones. Select a phase to open it.

NOW – 2030 · Foundation & awareness

  • Launch the Welcome Center, virtual community, outreach programmes and academies
  • Create the special economic zone and its infrastructure
  • Establish multiple high-impact programmes

2030 – 2040 · Space city & industry scale-up

  • Dedicated zones for advanced manufacturing, simulation, materials, space medicine and logistics
  • Develop residential and tourism programmes
  • Deepen industry and research partnerships

2040 – 2117 · Deep-space economy

  • Mature the UAE as a strategic hub for deep-space missions
  • Support the long-horizon Mars 2117 programme

The 2040 boundary is this concept’s own phasing. No UAE source publishes it as a milestone.

05 · Gateway

The Welcome Center

Proposed as the gateway to the concept city — a physical and virtual place where Emiratis, residents and visitors experience the nation’s journey to space and find how they can take part.

Immersive experiences
Interactive exhibits, mission simulators, planetary environments and a viewing gallery, designed to make the UAE’s space story tangible.
Education & talent pipeline
A bridge between accredited education providers, industry partners and government programmes, curating pathways into the sector.
Industry & R&D collaboration
A neutral collaboration zone connecting companies, investors and agencies to develop solutions for space from within the UAE.
