  • The Queue Board

    Running

    A small Melbourne server takes a reading every fifteen minutes from every NSW emergency department that publishes a live count. It ran on a laptop first and dropped readings; since the August cutover it has not missed one — 2,496 consecutive readings, 25 August to 19 September. 211,456 readings in total since July 2026.

    59Departments
    15Minutes apart
    365Days kept
    59 of the 176 emergency departments in NSW — the ones that publish a live count. The figure is people waiting for treatment; the feed carries no waiting time, so the page states none.
